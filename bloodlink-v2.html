KNOW BEFORE YOU DONATE

Your 10 Minutes Could Mean Someone’s Lifetime.

Who can donate?
  • Meet the minimum age requirement in your country.
  • Meet the weight requirement.
  • Haemoglobin within the accepted range.
  • Be in suitable general health.
  • Some medications, illnesses, procedures or travel can mean temporary or permanent deferral.
When should you not donate?
  • Fever or active infection.
  • Low haemoglobin.
  • Certain medical conditions.
  • Pregnancy and some postpartum periods.
  • Recent surgery or medical procedures.
  • Certain medications or temporary deferral conditions.
Why it matters
  • Helps patients needing surgery.
  • Supports accident and trauma care.
  • Helps people in cancer treatment.
  • Supports patients with severe anaemia and blood disorders.
  • Builds community participation and social responsibility.

Eligibility depends on local blood donation regulations and professional medical screening. This guide never replaces that screening.
